For the app team — what a UI must be able to show

Every screen is a view over on-chain state. These are the enums and events a standard UI has to render faithfully — never invent a status the chain does not have.

ThingStates / eventsUI meaning
AgreementCommitted → Fulfilled → Settled · Disputed → Resolved · Refundedan order card; the split line comes from SettlementSplit(fee, skim, net)
ListingActive · Paused · Closed · Cancelled · Challengeda product card; price + coarse cell only, never a street
PoolRaising → Active → Repaid · Defaulted · Cancelledprogress bars: raised/target and repaid/cap (1.25×)
Groupterms hash, members, treasury, proposals (open/executed/cancelled), pledgesrules BEFORE join; quorum % on every money button
RecoveryPending → Executed · Vetoed · Cancelleda countdown with a big VETO for the current key
GovernanceVoting → Queued → Executed · Rejected · Cancelled; stage ladderproposal pages with the rationale hash resolved to text
Vaultbalance, nonce, daily limit, pending payments (release/cancel)"your money answers to your key" — show the pending window

Design rule for every money moment: the buyer sees the total; the seller sees the split. Attestations are facts with an attester and a schema — show who said it and under what schema, never a bare checkmark.
